Research Highlights

Generating scalable embodied data without expensive hardware remains a central challenge for robot learning. We introduce Video2Robo, a framework for monocular video-to-robot data generation that converts a single human demonstration video into photorealistic, kinematically plausible robot training data. By leveraging 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) for explicit scene reconstruction and editable rendering, Video2Robo extracts task-relevant object trajectories and synthesizes diverse robot–object interactions, enabling effective policy learning and seamless transfer to real-world robots without teleoperation, simulators, or depth sensors.

  • Monocular 6D Object Understanding: An optimized 3DGS-rendering-based pose estimation method reconstructs task-relevant objects and tracks temporally consistent 6D trajectories from raw RGB videos, enabling skill extraction from relative object motion without embodied sensing.
  • Photorealistic & Editable 3DGS Rendering: The framework leverages explicit Gaussian scene editing to ensure high-fidelity rendering, physically plausible robot–object coordination, and elimination of simulator-induced artifacts.
  • Coupled Robot–Object Motion Synthesis: Extracted object trajectories are transformed into kinematically feasible robot actions via coordinated motion design, preserving task semantics under novel object arrangements.
  • Comprehensive Scene Augmentation: Video2Robo enhances generalization through pose, appearance, lighting, background, and view augmentation within a unified 3D representation, significantly increasing embodied data diversity.
  • Strong Generalization & Sim-to-Real Transfer: Policies trained purely on Video2Robo-generated data achieve superior generalization across object configurations and camera viewpoints, and transfer effectively to physical robot platforms.

Methods

Video2Robo takes one monocular human demonstration video as input, and the synthesized data can be directly utilized for training and deploying robot imitation learning policies. The overall framework of Video2Robo comprises three main components:
Scene and Skill Parsing performs Real2Sim functionality by extracting 3D object motion from the video and segmenting task skills.
Scalable Data Generation automatically synthesizes diverse robot demonstrations across varying scene configurations based on 3DGS representations.
Robot Learning and Deployment performs Sim2Real functionality by training visuomotor policies and deploying them for evaluation in both simulation and real-world environments.
